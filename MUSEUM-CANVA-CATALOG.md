## How to read this report

- **Cover stars** are read from the downloaded page-1 thumbnails (visually read with muse.read). Where page 1 was a placeholder/spread/another edition's cover, page 2 (and once page 3) was also read — noted as p.2/p.3.
- **Wall conflicts** = where Canva disagrees with the museum wall's current data (wall data came from store listings). Flagged for Jake/parent to resolve — nothing overwritten.
- Dates are UTC dates converted from Canva Unix timestamps. Edit URLs are Canva `canva.com/d/...` links.
