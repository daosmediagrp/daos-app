## 5. THE PRECURSOR EVENT — MAY 14, 2022 (Jake's link, added Nov 8, 2026)
Video: "The Deadly Art of Survival Event 5/14/22 Pt. 2 Chinatown NYC" — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kJO8Nzokmzo (uploaded Jun 11, 2022, 20m20s, 6,842 views, channel Outdoor Academe, NOT DAOS TV). Auto-captions exist (English) — transcript pulled Nov 8, 2026.
- Date/venue: May 14, 2022, Mariner's Temple Baptist Church, 25 Oliver Street, Two Bridges, Lower Manhattan (brownstone built 1845, Ionic columns, American Baptist Churches USA). Nearby: Alfred E. Smith House historic row houses (home of four-time NY governor Alfred E. Smith, 1928).
- Jake's note (Nov 8, 2026): this event "slowly changed to DAOS CON" — the early DAOS gathering that evolved into the DAOS CON series (CON 2 followed Sep–Oct 2022).
- Transcript facts: "On October 28, 2021 Nathan Ingram created the magazine The Deadly Art of Survival" — magazine founding date. Event spirit per the film: "humility, coming together as a family, breaking bread together, the legends being celebrated" — demonstrations plus seminars (women's seminar noted). "A great day for reunions, a lot of these guys hadn't seen each other in 10 plus years."
- People named on camera: Felix Vasquez, Bill Showman, Tom Keane, Ronald Duncan, Gregory Duncan (son of Ronald Duncan, who was Billy's teacher).
- Note: this is "Pt. 2" — a Part 1 exists on the same channel, not yet pulled.
