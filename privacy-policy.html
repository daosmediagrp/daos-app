Privacy Policy

THE DAOS APP — by The DAOS Foundation

Last updated: November 5, 2026

THE DAOS APP is the community app for The DAOS Foundation, a New York nonprofit that preserves, documents, and advances martial arts and music culture through DAOS Magazine, media channels, and live events. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and any display name you choose. Your password is handled by our login provider (Supabase) and is never stored in readable form by us.

Profile information. If you build a fighter or magazine profile, we collect what you enter: your name, martial arts style and rank, biography, and any photo you upload.

Content you post. Posts, comments, RSVPs, and votes you submit in the app, along with the date and time you submitted them.

Technical information. Basic diagnostic data needed to run the app, such as device type and app version, collected by our backend provider. We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or photos from your device library unless you choose to upload one.

How we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, show your profile, display your posts, and count votes.
  • To operate the community: review magazine feature applications and fighter profiles before they go live.
  • To run events: process your RSVPs for DAOS events such as the DAOS Party.
  • To keep the community safe: investigate reports of abusive or harmful content.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

Who we share it with

Supabase. Our backend provider stores account data, profiles, posts, and uploaded photos on our behalf. Supabase processes that data only to provide the service to us.

We do not sell your data. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone, and we do not share it with advertisers.

No ads or tracking in v1. THE DAOS APP has no third-party advertising and no cross-app tracking in version 1. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.

We will share information only if required by law, or to protect the safety of our community.

What is public

Approved fighter and magazine profiles, and posts you make in the community feed, are visible to other app users and may be shared outside the app. Your email address is never shown publicly. Voter email addresses used for voting are never shown publicly.

Community rules

By posting in THE DAOS APP you agree not to post anything unlawful, harassing, hateful, sexually explicit, or that infringes someone else's rights. Other users can report a post and block the person who posted it from the post menu. We review reports and may remove content or suspend accounts that break these rules.

Children

THE DAOS APP is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 18, please use the app with a parent or guardian's permission. If we learn that a child under 13 has created an account, we will delete it.

Your choices

Delete your account. To delete your account and your profile, posts, and photos, email daos@deadlyartofsurvival.com from the email address on the account with the subject "Delete my DAOS account". We will confirm and delete it within 30 days, except for information we must keep for legal or safety reasons.

Correct your information. You can edit your profile in the app at any time, or email us and we will correct it for you.

Contact us. Questions about this policy: email daos@deadlyartofsurvival.com or call 551-245-7120.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and post the new version here. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
