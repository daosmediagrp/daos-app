I Play Rocky: The Fight Before the Fight

DAOS Magazine · November 3, 2026

Long before the bell ever rang for Rocky Balboa, Sylvester Stallone was already in the fight of his life — and it was not in a ring. It was in a room full of studio executives who wanted his script and did not want him in it.

That is the story Peter Farrelly's I Play Rocky tells. A broke, unknown actor writes a script about a Philly club fighter, then refuses a fortune to hand it over unless he gets to play the lead himself. Anthony Ippolito — who taped his audition in his grandmother's basement after playing Al Pacino in The Offer — plays the young Stallone, alongside Stephan James as Carl Weathers and Matt Dillon as Stallone's father.

The film opens in select theaters November 6 and everywhere November 20, nearly 50 years after the original Rocky debuted. And the spirit is already in the gym: RDX's #MyFirstRound campaign, shared with DAOS by Karen Apablaza of Hudson West PR, is built around I Play Rocky-inspired gear for your first round.

Stallone did not win by knocking anybody out in that meeting. He won by refusing to move. That is the lesson.
